How to use Paperjet

Paperjet is a PDF toolkit for Android, iPhone and iPad. Open, convert, merge, split, sign, fill, edit and compress — everything runs on your device.

Quick start

  1. Tap Open and pick a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, image, EPUB or text file.
  2. Choose a tool from the home screen — Merge, Split, Sign, Fill, Edit, Compress or Convert.
  3. Save the result. It stays on your phone until you share or delete it.

No account, no upload. Your documents never leave the device.

1Open and convert files

Paperjet opens PDFs and converts other common formats to PDF:

  • Word (.doc, .docx), Excel (.xls, .xlsx), PowerPoint (.ppt, .pptx)
  • Images (JPG, PNG, HEIC and more)
  • EPUB and plain text

Tap Open, pick the file, and Paperjet converts it on the device. The original file is left alone — the converted PDF is saved beside your other Paperjet documents.

2Merge PDFs

Combine several PDFs into one.

  1. Tap Merge.
  2. Add the PDFs you want to combine.
  3. Drag to reorder, then tap Merge.

The source files are not changed. The merged PDF is saved as a new file you can rename.

3Split a PDF

Break one PDF into smaller ones.

  • By range: type the page ranges you want (for example 1-3, 5, 8-10).
  • Every N pages: split a long PDF into equal chunks.
  • Extract pages: pick individual pages to save as a new PDF.

4Sign a PDF

Add a signature to any page.

  1. Open the PDF and tap Sign.
  2. Draw your signature with your finger, or import a signature image.
  3. Drag it to the right spot and resize it. Tap Save.

If you save your signature, Paperjet keeps it on your device for next time. Delete it any time from the Sign screen.

5Fill forms

Tap a form field to type into it. Paperjet handles both interactive PDF forms and flat forms:

  • Interactive forms keep their fields when you save.
  • Flat forms (scanned pages) let you add text, checkmarks and dates anywhere on the page.

6Edit pages

Reorganize a PDF page by page.

  • Reorder, rotate or delete pages.
  • Insert blank pages or pages from another PDF.
  • Add text, highlights and shapes.

7Compress a PDF

Reduce the file size so it fits in email or upload limits.

  1. Tap Compress and pick a PDF.
  2. Choose a quality level — Paperjet shows the expected size before saving.
  3. Tap Save. The original is kept; the smaller copy is saved separately.

8Images to PDF

Turn photos or scans into a single PDF.

  1. Tap Images to PDF.
  2. Pick the pictures, drag to reorder, and choose page size.
  3. Save. Each image becomes one page.

9Recents and clearing data

Recent documents appear on the home screen so you can jump back in.

  • Remove one: long-press a document in Recents.
  • Clear all: tap Clear on the home screen.
  • Erase everything: uninstall the app.

10Ads and privacy

Paperjet is free and shows ads from Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network. The ad providers never see your documents, signatures or form entries.

  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland, Paperjet shows Google's consent form before any ads load. You can change your choice later with Ad privacy choices on the home screen.
  • On iPhone and iPad, you can refuse app tracking in the system prompt or Settings.

11FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Paperjet has no sign-up and no server.
Where are my files saved?
On your device, in Paperjet's own folder. Use Share to send them elsewhere.
Does Paperjet work offline?
Yes. Everything except ads runs offline.
Can I remove ads?
Not yet. Ads keep Paperjet free.
