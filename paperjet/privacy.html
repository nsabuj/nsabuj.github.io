Paperjet

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

The short version

  • Your documents never leave your device. PDFs, images, signatures and everything you type into forms are processed only on your phone or tablet. We don't upload, read, store or share them.
  • No account and no app server. Paperjet has no sign-up, and we don't run a server that receives your data.
  • Ads. Paperjet is free and shows ads from Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network. These ad providers collect some device and usage data to show and measure ads. You can choose personalised or non-personalised ads, and on iPhone and iPad you can refuse tracking.
  • Update check. The app asks Google Play or the App Store whether a newer version exists.

1. Who we are

Paperjet (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a PDF toolkit for Android, iPhone and iPad published by Md Nur Uddin Sabuj, [postal address]. For anything in this policy, contact us at nur.uudevs@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

Everything you do in Paperjet happens on your device. This includes:

  • PDFs and other files you open, import or convert (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, EPUB, text and more);
  • documents you create by merging, splitting, signing, filling, adding text, editing pages or compressing;
  • your handwritten or uploaded signature, if you choose to save it;
  • text you enter into PDF forms;
  • app settings, such as your language, reading mode and list of recent documents.

This data is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and our ad and update providers have no access to it. Your original files are never changed: Paperjet always works on a copy.

How to delete it: remove a document from Recents (long-press it) or use Clear to remove all of them, delete your saved signature on the Sign screen, or uninstall the app to erase all of its data.

3. Permissions

  • Photos / files: used only when you choose a picture (for “Images to PDF” or to upload a signature) or pick a document to open. Paperjet can only see the items you select.
  • Internet: used only for ads and the update check described below. All PDF processing works offline.
  • Advertising identifier (Android advertising ID / Apple IDFA): used by the ad providers, as described in section 4. On iPhone and iPad it's only used if you tap “Allow” on Apple's tracking prompt.

Paperjet does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

4. Advertising

Paperjet is free because it shows ads: a banner on some screens and, at most once every few minutes, a full-screen ad after a task finishes. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Some of them are delivered by Meta Audience Network through AdMob mediation. These companies act as independent controllers of the data they collect.

What the ad providers may collect

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (advertising ID / IDFA, if allowed) and IP addressShowing ads, measuring them, frequency capping, fraud prevention; personalised ads only with your consent

The ad providers never receive your documents, file names, file contents, signatures or form entries.

Your choices

  • Europe (EEA), UK and Switzerland: before any ads load, Paperjet shows Google’s consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices at the bottom of the home screen.
  • US states with privacy laws: where required, the same form lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted ads.
  • iPhone and iPad: Apple’s “Allow tracking” prompt decides whether your IDFA can be used. Choose “Ask App Not to Track” to refuse. You can change this later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads (the wording varies by device).

If you refuse personalised ads, you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your interests.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy · Meta Privacy Policy · Meta ad preferences.

5. Update check

When you open the home screen, Paperjet may ask the store whether a newer version is available. On Android this uses Google Play; on iPhone and iPad it uses Apple’s App Store lookup service, which receives the app’s identifier. If an update exists, Paperjet reminds you once a day until you install it. Like any internet request, the store receives your IP address. Google’s and Apple’s privacy policies apply to these requests.

6. What we don’t do

  • We don’t have user accounts and don’t collect your name, email or phone number.
  • We don’t use our own analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don’t sell your personal information. Data collected by the ad providers is handled under their own policies and the choices described in section 4.

7. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Processing your documents on your device needs no legal basis on our side, because we never receive them. For advertising, personalised ads and the use of identifiers are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security rely on legitimate interests: keeping the app free and its ads safe. The update check relies on our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and working.

8. Data retention

Data stored by the app stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall Paperjet. Data collected by Google, Meta or the app stores is kept according to their own retention policies.

9. Children

Paperjet is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected by our ad providers, contact us and we will help you request deletion.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. We hold no personal data about you on our side. To exercise these rights for advertising data, use the choices in section 4 or contact Google and Meta directly. You can also contact us at nur.uudevs@gmail.com, and we will help.

11. Security

Your documents stay in the app’s private storage, protected by your device’s own security (such as your screen lock and encryption). Network requests made by the ad and store services use encrypted connections.

12. International transfers

Google, Meta and the app stores may process advertising and update-check data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how Paperjet handles data, we will update this page and its effective date, and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or the store listing.
