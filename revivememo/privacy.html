Revivememo

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

In short
  • Revivememo edits your photos and videos on your device. We do not run servers that receive your media.
  • We do not collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Optional features that use the internet (AI pack downloads, purchases, Google Drive export) only run when you choose them.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Revivememo mobile app for Android and iOS (“the app”, “we”, “us”). If you have questions, contact us at nur.uudevs@gmail.com.

2. Your photos and videos

When you choose a photo or video, the app makes a private copy inside its own storage on your device so that your original is never changed. Your edits, captions, dates and album information are stored only on your device.

  • Deleting a project or album in the app removes those copies.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
  • Files you export are saved only where you choose: your photo library, or an app you pick in the share sheet.

3. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store or sell personal information, usage data, device identifiers or location. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Optional features that use the internet

On-device AI packs

If you choose to download an AI feature (such as AI upscale or AI colourise), your device downloads a model file from our public download host on GitHub. Like any download, GitHub can see your IP address and the time of the request, under GitHub’s privacy statement. The model then runs entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded for AI processing. You can remove each AI pack at any time in Settings.

Purchases

Revivememo Pro is sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play, which process your payment under their own privacy policies. The app only checks with the store, on your device, whether you own Pro. We never see your payment details or your store account.

Google Drive export

If you choose to export to Google Drive, you sign in with Google and grant permission for the app to create and manage only the files it creates in your Drive (the drive.file permission). The app cannot see your other Drive files.

  • What is accessed: your Google account sign-in (to obtain an access token) and the files and folders Revivememo creates in your Drive.
  • How it is used: only to upload the photos, videos and scans you select, to the folders you choose, and to avoid uploading the same file twice.
  • Where it goes: files travel directly from your device to your own Google Drive over an encrypted connection. They never pass through our servers, and we cannot access your Drive.
  • Storage: sign-in tokens are kept on your device by Google’s sign-in library. Uploaded files use your Google Drive storage quota.
  • Revoking access: sign out in the app’s Settings, or remove Revivememo at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Revivememo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell or share it, and no person reads it.

6. Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. When you use the system share sheet, the file goes to the app you choose, under that app’s policy.

7. Security and retention

Your media and project data stay in the app’s private storage on your device, protected by your device’s security. We keep no copies because we never receive your data. Downloaded AI packs are checked against a fixed fingerprint before they are used.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Your choices and rights

All of your data is under your control on your device: you can view, export or delete it in the app at any time. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf, but you are welcome to contact us with any request or question.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
