How to use ThreatPick

ThreatPick checks your phone for fake apps, pop-up adware and dangerous files. Every scan runs on your phone, so your apps and files never leave it.

Quick start

  1. Run an app scan.
  2. Run a file scan.
  3. Review anything flagged, then uninstall or quarantine it.

Turn on real-time alerts if you want ThreatPick to keep checking in the background.

1Scan your apps

The app scan looks at every app installed on your phone and flags the ones worth a closer look:

  • Fake or modified apps: copies of popular apps whose signing certificate doesn't match the real developer's.
  • Sideloaded apps: apps installed from outside Google Play.
  • Powerful permissions: apps that hold permissions that could be misused.

If you decide to remove an app, ThreatPick opens Android's uninstall screen for it. Android always asks you to confirm.

A flag doesn't always mean an app is harmful. An app you installed on purpose from a trusted website will show as sideloaded. Keep it if you trust where it came from.

2Find the app behind pop-up ads

Seeing full-screen ads on your home screen or on top of other apps? The pop-up ad culprit finder uses Android's usage history to show which app was running when the ads appeared, so you know what to uninstall.

This needs Usage access. ThreatPick asks for it the first time you use the finder.

Use it soon after a pop-up appears. The closer in time, the easier it is to spot the app responsible.

3Scan your files

The file scan checks your shared storage, including Downloads, WhatsApp and Telegram media, photos and documents. It looks for:

  • Dangerous app installers (APK files)
  • Programs disguised as photos or documents
  • Damaged files

Scanning shared storage needs All files access. It's optional, and ThreatPick only asks when you start a file scan.

4Quarantine, restore or delete

Move a suspicious file into quarantine to take it out of the way without deleting it. From quarantine you can:

  • Restore the file if it turns out to be safe.
  • Delete it for good.

Nothing is deleted unless you choose to delete it.

5Turn on real-time alerts

With alerts on, ThreatPick checks your phone in the background about every 30 minutes and notifies you when a new suspicious app or dangerous download appears.

Alerts need the Notifications permission.

7Remove ads

ThreatPick is free and supported by ads. A one-time Remove ads purchase turns ads off for good, and follows your Google account to your other phones.

8FAQ

Are my apps or files uploaded?
No. Scans run on your phone. Your app list, files and scan results are never uploaded. ThreatPick downloads updated threat lists, but never sends your data.
Do I need an account?
No. There's no account or sign-up.
Which languages does it support?
ThreatPick is available in 8 languages.

Good to know: ThreatPick adds an extra layer of protection. No security app can catch every threat, so keep installing apps from trusted sources and keep your phone up to date.
