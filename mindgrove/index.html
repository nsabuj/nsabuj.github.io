Mindgrove

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

In short: Mindgrove has no accounts and never sends your game progress anywhere. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (with Meta Audience Network as an additional ad source), and those ad providers use your device's advertising identifier and related data to show and measure ads. On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking.

Who we are

Mindgrove ("the app") is a spatial-memory puzzle game for Android and iOS (iPhone and iPad) published by Md Nur Uddin Sabuj ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (levels completed and stars earned) and your settings (language, difficulty and forest time) only on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the app, or by deleting (uninstalling) the app.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personal information. The app does not include analytics or tracking tools of our own, and we do not operate any servers that receive data from the app.

Advertising

The app shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may also fill ads from Meta Audience Network through mediation. To show, personalize (where permitted) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these providers may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier: Google's Android advertising ID on Android, or on iOS, Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • other device identifiers, such as Apple's Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) on iOS, used to measure ads and prevent fraud;
  • information about your device and the app, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • diagnostic information about the ad SDKs, such as crash logs.

The app asks ad providers to show only ads rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (Google's "PG" rating or lower). Age-restricted ads, such as those rated for teens or mature audiences, are not requested.

This data is collected directly by the ad providers under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store it ourselves, beyond the aggregated, non-identifying reports the ad networks provide to publishers:

  • Google: Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Meta Audience Network: Meta Privacy Policy

Your consent and choices

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads.

On iPhone and iPad, the app uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt to ask whether ads may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared with ad providers and you will see less personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and you can manage Apple's own advertising settings in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

On either platform, you can delete or uninstall the app at any time, which stops all data collection by the app and its ad providers.

Rewarded ads are always optional. They are only shown when you choose to watch one, for example to replay a trail.

Children

Mindgrove is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help address it.

Data security and retention

Data sent to ad providers is transmitted over encrypted connections. Game progress stays on your device until you reset it or delete the app. How long ad providers retain data is described in their policies linked above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google and Meta through the links above. You are also welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the app's features change. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
