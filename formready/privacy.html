Who we are

FormReady Fit (the "app") is published by Md Nur Uddin Sabuj ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the information described in this policy. The app is the mobile version of the FormReady tools at formready.tools. You can reach us at nur.uudevs@gmail.com.

Your photos and files

The app only reads a picture when you choose it, through your phone's photo picker or the Files picker. What happens next:

  • Processing is local. Compressing, resizing, converting, cropping, checking file rules and removing metadata all happen on your device. There is no upload and no server that receives your pictures.
  • Metadata is read on the device. The metadata remover looks inside your photo (for example camera model, date and GPS location) so it can show you what it found and remove it. That information stays on your phone.
  • Temporary results. Finished pictures are kept in the app's temporary storage so you can share or save them. They are deleted every time the app starts, and you can delete them at any time from About → Clear temporary files now.
  • You decide what leaves the app. A result is shared only when you tap Share (your phone's share sheet opens and you pick where it goes) or saved to your gallery only when you tap Save.
  • Your original photos are never changed.

The app does not collect your name, email address, contact details, precise or approximate location, or the contents of your files.

Ads and advertising data

FormReady Fit is free and is supported by advertising. It shows banner and full-screen (interstitial) ads served by Google AdMob, using Google's Mobile Ads SDK.

Ad mediation (Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network)

AdMob can run mediation: it asks several ad networks to compete to fill each ad slot so that the best-paying ad is shown. FormReady Fit uses AdMob mediation with Meta's adapter built into the app, so it can show ads from Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms, Inc. and Meta Platforms Ireland Limited) in addition to Google's own ads. Which network's ad you see depends on which one wins the auction for that slot. When Meta's ads are requested, Meta's software in the app may collect the same kinds of data listed below (advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions) and uses it under Meta's Privacy Policy, which also explains how to manage the ads you see from Meta. Other ad networks may be added to mediation in future; they are covered by this section in the same way, and this policy will be updated to name them.

To select, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's software on your device may collect and use:

Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS, where you allow it)Showing and measuring ads, limiting how often you see the same ad

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not by us. We do not receive it, we do not sell it, and your photos and their contents are never provided to any ads software. Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and read Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app shows Google's consent form the first time it opens, before any ad is requested. Meta's ads are requested only after this step, and your choice applies to all ad partners in mediation, including Meta. If a region does not require consent, ads may be personalised using the information above. On iPhone, the app also asks for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency rules before using your advertising identifier.

Third-party code in the app that connects to the internet: Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google LLC) and the Meta Audience Network adapter and SDK (Meta Platforms, Inc.). FormReady Fit has no analytics service and no crash-reporting service of its own, so these two are the only third-party code in the app that communicates over the internet.

Permissions

  • Photos and files: you pick pictures with the system picker, so the app does not get access to your whole gallery.
  • Save to Photos: used only to write a finished picture to your gallery when you tap Save.
  • Internet and network state: used by the ads software and to open links. Picture processing does not need the internet.
  • Advertising ID (Android AD_ID): used for advertising as described above. You can switch it off in your phone's settings.

Links to other sites

The app links to our website and to official government pages that publish photo rules. When you open a link, your browser loads that site, which has its own privacy practices. We do not control those sites.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad consent: where your region requires it, About → Ad privacy choices reopens Google's consent form.
  • Reset or limit your advertising ID: on Android go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (delete or reset advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation). On iPhone go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete data held on your device: use Clear temporary files now or uninstall the app. We hold no personal data about you on any server of our own.
  • Your legal rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or laws in California and other US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your personal data, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, or to Meta where its ads are shown, through the links above. You can also write to us at the address below and we will help where we can.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising ourselves; advertising data is handled by Google as described above.

Children

FormReady Fit is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Security and retention

Because pictures are processed on your device and not sent to us, they are protected by your phone's own security. Temporary results are removed each time the app starts. We keep no copies of your pictures and no user profiles. Advertising data held by Google is kept according to Google's policies.
