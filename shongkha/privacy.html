Shongkha: Number Game

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Shongkha: Number Game app (“the app”, “we”) handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is published by Md Nur Uddin Sabuj.

In short: there are no accounts and no in-app purchases. Your games, settings and diamonds stay on your device. When you play with friends, the name you choose and a room code pass through our room service and are deleted automatically within 2 hours. The app shows ads from Google AdMob (with Meta Audience Network), which collect device and usage information as described below.

1. Information stored only on your device

  • The player name you enter.
  • Your settings (language, sound, haptics, numerals, reduced motion).
  • Your diamond balance, daily check-in and rewarded-ad counts.
  • A random identifier created by the app so you can rejoin a friends’ room after a dropped connection.

This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Playing with friends

When you create or join a room:

  • Room service. Your player name, the room code, the app’s random identifier and game messages are sent to our room service, which runs on Cloudflare. Like any internet service, it receives your IP address to deliver the connection. Room data is deleted automatically within 2 hours after the room was last used.
  • Other players. The players in your room see your player name, your moves and your scores. To keep the game fast, phones in a room try to connect to each other directly (peer-to-peer). A direct connection lets the other phones in the room see your IP address.

We use this information only to run the game. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or keep it after the room expires.

3. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads (banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads) through Google AdMob, which can deliver ads from Meta Audience Network. These providers may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostics about ad performance.

They use this information to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where permitted and you have agreed, personalise ads. Their own policies apply: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Meta Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are personalised. On iOS, the app asks for permission before your device’s advertising identifier can be used for tracking.

4. Store services

After you finish a match, the app may ask the app store to show its rating prompt (Google Play In-App Review or the App Store rating dialog). This is handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies; we do not see whether you rated the app or what you wrote.

6. Security

Connections to the room service use HTTPS and secure WebSockets, and direct connections between players are encrypted (DTLS). No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we limit what we handle to what the game needs.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your choices

  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your consent choices.
  • Local data: clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.
  • Room data: leave the room; its data is deleted automatically within 2 hours.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Contact us to make a request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
