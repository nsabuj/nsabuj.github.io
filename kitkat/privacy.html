Kit Kat Jodhu Modhu

Privacy policy · Effective 25 October 2026

In short

Kit Kat Jodhu Modhu ("the app") is a card game made by Md Nur Uddin Sabuj ("we", "us"). You don't need an account, and we never ask for your email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. To run online games and keep your diamonds, we use a random ID that isn't linked to who you are. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network, and those companies collect some device information as described below.

Information kept on your phone

The app saves these on your phone only:

  • the player name and picture you choose for online games
  • your settings (language, sound, vibration)
  • your game statistics
  • a random wallet ID and a random player ID, created by the app
  • the last address you typed for Same Wi-Fi play

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Information on our server

Online play and diamonds use our server, which runs on Cloudflare.

  • Your diamond wallet. When you first open the app, the server creates a wallet with a random ID. It stores only that ID, your diamond balance, when the wallet was created, when you last earned diamonds from a video, and a short list of recent transactions so nothing is counted twice. It contains no name or contact details.
  • Online games. When you play with friends or with anyone, your player name, picture and moves pass through the server to the other players in your game. The server keeps a temporary copy of the game so it can carry on if the host leaves. That copy is deleted automatically once the room closes.
  • Connection data. Like any internet service, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver connections and may keep short-lived technical logs for security and troubleshooting. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Playing alone against the computer, and Same Wi-Fi games between phones, don't use our server for gameplay.

Advertising

The app is free and paid for by ads. We use Google AdMob, which can show ads from Meta Audience Network through mediation. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, these companies may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA, if you allow it)
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads

When you watch a rewarded video, Google tells our server that the video finished, with your random wallet ID, so we can add your diamonds.

Your choices: if you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. On iPhone, the app asks for permission to track, and the game works the same if you say no. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Meta's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or files.
  • There are no in-app purchases. Diamonds can't be bought or exchanged for money.

Security

Information between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS and secure WebSockets).

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

Game copies on the server are deleted when the room closes. Your wallet stays on the server so your diamonds aren't lost. It isn't linked to your identity, so we can't look it up by name. To have it deleted, email us with your diamond balance and roughly when you installed the app, and we'll find and remove it. You can also uninstall the app to delete everything stored on your phone.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
