Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app “GenAI Academy” (package com.genaiacademy.app)

In short:

• GenAI Academy has no accounts, no backend and no analytics of its own. Your learning progress is stored only on your device.

• The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device and usage information to show and measure ads. This is the only data collection in the app.

• You can reset your learning data in the app at any time and manage ad choices in the app and in your device settings.

02Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following in a private database on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server we operate:

  • Your learning progress: which lessons you have read and passed, quiz scores and attempts, and dates.
  • Your preferences: for example, your chosen colour theme.
  • Your daily-challenge answers and streak, and which credentials you have earned.

We have no way to see this information. It is removed when you uninstall the app, or earlier if you choose Profile → Reset all learning progress.

03Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files, microphone or precise location. The app’s “lab” experiments are pre-written simulations that run entirely on your device; no AI service is called and nothing you do in them is transmitted.
